jFlix Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

jFlix is a media player application that allows users to import and play authorized streaming playlists, including M3U playlists. This Privacy Policy explains how jFlix handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

jFlix does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address to use the application.

jFlix does not intentionally collect personal information from users through the application.

2. Playlist and Channel Information

When you import an M3U playlist, jFlix may process information contained in that playlist, including:

  • Channel names
  • Channel categories
  • Streaming URLs
  • Channel logo URLs
  • Other metadata contained in the playlist

This information is used solely to organize and play the content within the application.

3. Local Storage

jFlix stores your imported channel information, favorites, and related application data locally on your device so that your settings and channel collection can remain available when you reopen the application.

This information is not uploaded to a jFlix server.

4. Streaming Content

jFlix does not provide television channels or streaming content itself. The application allows users to provide their own streaming playlist URLs.

When you play a channel, the application connects to the streaming service associated with the URL provided by you. That service may independently collect information according to its own privacy policy.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate rights and permissions to access and view content through their playlists.

5. Internet Connections

jFlix requires an internet connection to download playlists, retrieve channel information and logos, and play online streaming content.

Network requests may be sent directly to the servers associated with the URLs provided by the user.

6. Analytics and Advertising

The current version of jFlix does not intentionally include third-party advertising or analytics services.

7. Third-Party Services

jFlix may connect to third-party servers when you use streaming URLs, playlist URLs, or image URLs supplied by you.

These third-party services operate independently of jFlix and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

jFlix does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

Because jFlix does not operate a user account system or maintain a central database of user playlists, most playlist and channel information remains locally on the user's device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as jFlix develops new features or changes how information is handled. Any updated policy will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or jFlix, please contact us using the support information provided on the jFlix Support page.
